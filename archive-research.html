How ROSEI Works

Breaking Down
the Silos

Energy problems don't respect disciplinary boundaries. ROSEI draws from every corner of Johns Hopkins to build research teams no single school could assemble alone.

"At Hopkins, the problem determines the team. ROSEI exists to connect researchers and point them towards the hardest questions in energy."

Our Model

Discovery to
Deployment

ROSEI doesn't stop at publication. We move findings from the lab bench to the grid, the market, and the policy floor — because knowledge that stays on the shelf doesn't power anything.

Discovery
Fundamental research across disciplines
Development
Applied prototyping & proof-of-concept
Translation
Partnerships, licensing & spinouts
Policy
Briefings, testimony & state collaborations
Deployment
Real-world impact on grids, markets & communities
